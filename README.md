# Tropical Lagoon Outpost

A small first-person island: walk, swim, dive, take the boat out, change the weather and the time of day. Early playtest build.

**Play:** https://luckey19871005.github.io/lagoon-outpost/

- Computer: keyboard and mouse. Click PLAY; Esc pauses and opens the settings.
- Phone: hold it sideways. Left thumb moves, right thumb looks, the buttons do the rest.

一个第一人称的热带小岛：可以走、游泳、潜水、开小船，换天气和时间。早期试玩版。

- 电脑：键盘加鼠标，点 PLAY 开始，Esc 暂停和设置。
- 手机：横过来玩。左手拇指走路，右手拇指转视角，其余看按钮。
